## Review log

For each problem, record:

- Whether the first approach was correct.
- The key invariant or state transition.
- Time and space complexity.
- Edge case missed, if any.
- Whether the final solution passed local tests.
- One specific action to prevent the same mistake.

Do not count an accepted solution as fully reviewed until you can explain its
invariant and complexity without looking at the code.
